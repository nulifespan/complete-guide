Q: Why does the same prescription cost different amounts at different pharmacies?

A: Pricing for identical medications can vary based on the pharmacy's supplier agreements, discount programs, and whether insurance or a discount card applies — comparing before filling is often worth it.

Q: Is a generic medication the same as the brand-name version?

A: Generics are required to contain the same active ingredient at the same dosage as their brand-name counterpart; the difference usually comes down to inactive ingredients and price.

Q: What exactly is sensory memory?

A: It's the brief initial stage where the brain holds onto raw sensory input sound, sight, touch for a fraction of a second before deciding what's worth paying attention to.

Q: How does sensory memory affect focus?

A: Since it determines what gets passed along to short-term attention, how well it functions can influence how easily someone notices and retains details in a busy environment.

Q: Can prescription costs change even with the same insurance plan?

A: Yes formulary updates, pharmacy benefit manager negotiations, and the specific pharmacy involved can all shift the price even when the plan itself hasn't changed.
